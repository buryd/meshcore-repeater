# Companion bill of materials

The parts table is in [BOM.xlsx](BOM.xlsx), sheet **Companion BOM**. Open that file in Excel or Google Sheets. Markdown cannot hold a spreadsheet.

One handheld MeshCore client for 100 Hickory Bend Drive. The radio is a XIAO nRF52840 with a Wio-SX1262. The build order is in [COMPANION-CONSTRUCTION.md](COMPANION-CONSTRUCTION.md).

Shelf prices on 10 November 2026. Shipping is extra. The blade, the pigtail, and the box are all RP-SMA, so the blade screws onto the bulkhead. Total before shipping: **$49.99**.

The kit’s short antenna stays in the spare-parts bag. The blade is the antenna that mounts outside the box. Cut the JST plug off the Adafruit cell, or solder its leads, only after the meter shows which wire is positive.

The repeater parts list is on sheet **Repeater BOM** in the same workbook.
