# Warner Robins repeater plan

The tables are in [DEPLOYMENT.xlsx](DEPLOYMENT.xlsx). Open that file in Excel or Google Sheets. Markdown cannot hold a spreadsheet.

Pulled from map.meshcore.io on 10 November 2026. 71,011 uploaded nodes. Centered on 32.621° N, 83.600° W. Seven repeater listings within 10 miles. None at the city point. Preset 910.525 MHz, 62.5 kHz, SF7, coding rate 5. K3JSW uses coding rate 8 and still hears coding rate 5.

Map: https://map.meshcore.io/?lat=32.621&lon=-83.600&zoom=11

## Sheets in DEPLOYMENT.xlsx

1. **Repeaters** — one row per uploaded repeater within 10 miles of the city, plus the proposed city repeater as the first data row. Columns: Node, Miles from city, Direction, From the house, Last map update, What to do.
2. **Plan** — one column per place (West side, South end, City and north, House, Second repeater). Rows: On the map now, Recommended change, Place, Radio, Antenna, Mount.

Recommended change: build **Proposed Warner Robins city repeater** between Watson Boulevard and Russell Parkway, west of the Robins AFB fence. Both Watson and Russell run east–west; Watson is the northern strip, Russell is south of it. Leave the repeaters already on the map. Do not build at 100 Hickory Bend Drive. Add a second repeater only if a walk north of Watson toward Macon still hears nobody.

The next nodes outside the Repeaters sheet are two M4GRY_MAC pins about 16 miles north and CannonBall274 about 18 miles northeast. They do not cover Warner Robins. The build is in [CONSTRUCTION.md](CONSTRUCTION.md).
